--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. HARDWARE CONSTRAINTS & WARNINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* ADC2 Pins (GPIO 13, 14, 25, 26, 27):
  - These pins are on ADC2. Since WiFi is used, they CANNOT be used for Analog Input.
  - They ARE safe to use for Digital Output (Relays, PWM) or Digital Input.
  - We use 13/14 for Relays to save ADC1 pins for sensors.

* GPIO 16 & 17 (Not Used for Relays anymore):
  - Avoid using these if possible; they conflict with PSRAM on WROVER modules.
  - RFID RST is on 17 by default; move to another pin if using WROVER.

* GPIO 5 (RFID SDA/SS):
  - Strapping Pin (VSPICS0). Must not be held LOW during boot.
  - RFID Module SDA pin is high-impedance input, so this is OK (Verified).
