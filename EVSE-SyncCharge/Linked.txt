The Energy Saga Continues — Building Smarter Self-Consumption with EVSE-SyncCharge

Most "smart" DIY EV chargers are just Wi-Fi relays in a fancy box. I decided to build something that actually deserves the name.

Meet EVSE-SyncCharge. It's not just a charger; it's a full-stack Energy Management Gateway, built from the ground up for real-time control and safety. The goal? To dynamically match your EV charging with solar production and household load — so you use your own energy first and avoid paying grid prices when you don't have to.

🚀 Introducing EVSE-SyncCharge — an embedded project engineered for real-world EV charging control. 
It implements the full SAE J1772 / IEC 61851 stack, properly negotiates the pilot signal, and enforces onboard charger limits. Because EV charging can be done better.
🌟 Core Features
☀️ Charge from excess solar — prioritize what you generate
⚡ Real-time load balancing — react instantly to household demand
🔌 Integrate with MQTT & OCPP backends
🧠 Native Home Assistant integration — no YAML required
🔄 Dynamic 1-phase / 3-phase switching (1.4 kW → 22 kW) with fully compliant discharge interlocks
🔒 Safety-first architecture — from hardware to firmware


⚙️ Project Status: Active Development
EVSE-SyncCharge is an ongoing project. It has recently passed the alpha phase as a working proof of concept — the system is functional and actively tested, but development is still in progress.

Detailed schematics, hardware documentation, and additional technical information will be released gradually as available free time permits. This is a passion project, built properly, not rushed.
